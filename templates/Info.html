VeriScan field guide / 05

Understand the signal.

VeriScan combines computer-vision landmarks, audio features, and language signals to create a live telemetry view. These metrics describe patterns detected by the system; they do not reveal a person’s private thoughts, intentions, truthfulness, or character.

01 / Live metrics

What each signal means

AU04Brow contraction

Estimates brow-lowering movement from facial landmarks. Lighting, camera angle, glasses, and expression can affect the signal.

AU12Smile movement

Tracks mouth-corner movement as an observable facial action. It is not a direct measure of happiness or sincerity.

POSTUREShift speed

Measures changes in tracked body landmarks over time. Larger movement can reflect many ordinary contexts, including adjustment or activity.

FIDGETMovement score

Summarizes short-term movement in tracked facial landmarks. It is sensitive to camera stability and should be read as a pattern, not a diagnosis.

VOICEPitch and volume

Reports acoustic features such as approximate pitch and loudness. Microphone distance, background noise, and speaking style influence them.

STRESS CUESCombined estimate

Combines configured visual, vocal, and transcript cue contributions with smoothing. It estimates observable cues and cannot measure internal stress.

02 / System flow

How the live system works

01 / CAPTURE

Camera and microphone

Media stays in the browser while the system samples frames and audio features.

02 / TRACK

Landmark detection

MediaPipe tracks body and face landmarks to calculate movement and facial action values.

03 / SMOOTH

Stabilized metrics

Exponential smoothing reduces sudden noise so the dashboard is easier to interpret.

04 / REVIEW

Human interpretation

Users compare signals with context instead of treating a single score as a conclusion.

03 / Video analysis

How uploaded video is analyzed

Video analysis uses Gemini to review the uploaded clip and return structured context. The model is instructed to describe observable audiovisual content, signal quality, cue timelines, and limitations.

Upload and inspect

The server validates the file type and size, uploads a temporary copy for analysis, and removes the temporary local file afterward.

Structured output

Results include a summary, observations, observable metrics, a cue estimate, a timeline, a signal profile, and recording quality.

Important boundary: Behavioral or vocal cues cannot establish guilt, innocence, deception, identity, intent, or mental state. Results are assistive observations and require human review, especially for consequential decisions.
